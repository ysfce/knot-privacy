Last updated: 21 September 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

This policy explains what information the mobile game Knot ("the game"), made by Nodalis Games ("we", "us"), handles, and why. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not receive any personal information from it.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (the level you are on, the levels you have solved and the hearts you earned on them) and your settings (language, vibration and sound) are saved only in the game's own storage on your device. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Advertising

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear as full-screen ads after some levels, and as optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for an extra hint.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • information about the ads you see and interact with.

We do not see or store this information. It is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

4. In-app purchases

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so that it can stop showing ads.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and security

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to retain or secure on our side. Information collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to its processing. As we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data can be made to Google, and you can manage your ad settings as described in section 3. You are always welcome to contact us with questions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the game gains new features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
